About SteadyDollar

SteadyDollar is for people whose money runs out before the month does. No lectures about lattes, no “just earn more.” Just simple methods that work when every dollar is already spoken for:

  • Budget one paycheck at a time, so bills never land on the wrong week.
  • Pay off debt in a clear order, with a real debt-free date.
  • Save for the “surprise” bills that aren’t really surprises.

Every guide is free. If you’d like a spreadsheet that does the math for you, our templates work in Excel and Google Sheets.

Questions or ideas for a guide? Reply to any of our emails.
